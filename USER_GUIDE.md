# MathGameApp Web — End User Guide

## Welcome
MathGameApp Web is a math practice game designed for students. Use this guide to play sessions, track progress, and get help when needed.

## 1. Signing In
1. Open the app in your browser.
2. Click **Login** and enter your username and password.
3. If you do not have an account, use the **Register** page to create one.
4. If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password** and follow the prompts.

## 2. Starting a Game
1. After logging in, go to the **Start Game** or **Mode Selection** screen.
2. Choose a game mode:
   - **Computational**: arithmetic operations like addition, subtraction, multiplication, division.
   - **Algebra**: solve simple equations.
   - **Binary**: convert numbers between binary and decimal.
3. Choose a difficulty level:
   - **Level 1** is easiest, and levels increase in challenge through **Level 5**.
4. Click **Start Game** to begin the session.

## 3. How the Session Works
- Each session gives you a fixed number of questions.
- The game shows one question at a time.
- You enter your answer in the input field.
- There are three main options:
  - **Submit**: send your answer and move to the next question.
  - **Skip Question**: skip the current question if you're unsure.
  - **Show Solution**: view the correct answer and explanation after answering or skipping.

## 4. Answering Questions
1. Read the question carefully.
2. Type your answer in the answer box.
3. Press the **Submit** button.
4. The app immediately tells you if your answer is correct.
5. If your answer is incorrect, use **Show Solution** to learn the right method.

## 5. Skipping Questions
- Use **Skip Question** when you do not want to answer a question.
- Skipping saves the question as skipped and reveals the correct answer.
- Skipped questions count separately from answered and unanswered questions.

## 6. Using Hints and Solutions
- After a question is answered or skipped, the app provides a **Show Solution** button.
- The solution explains how to reach the correct answer.
- Use this feature to learn from mistakes and reinforce understanding.

## 7. Quitting Mid-Session
- If you need to stop before finishing, click **Quit**.
- The game saves your session progress.
- You can review what you completed in the session summary.

## 8. Session Summary
After a session ends, you see a summary screen showing:
- Total score
- Number of correct answers
- Number of skipped questions
- Number of unanswered questions
- Time taken

This summary helps you understand your performance and what to improve.

## 9. Reviewing Past Sessions
- Go to the **History** or **Home** page.
- The app shows your most recent sessions.
- Click a session to view details and review the questions you answered.

## 10. Best Practices
- Take your time reading each question.
- Use **Show Solution** after incorrect or skipped questions.
- Play regularly to improve.
- If you are an educator-linked student, your teacher may review your progress.

## 11. Need Help?
- Use **Forgot Password** if you cannot log in.
- Contact your teacher or admin if your account has issues.
- If the app does not load or you get an error, refresh the page and try again.

## 12. Quick Reference
- **Login**: access your account
- **Register**: create a new account
- **Mode Selection**: choose game mode and level
- **Submit**: answer a question
- **Skip Question**: skip a question
- **Show Solution**: see the correct answer and explanation
- **Quit**: end the current session early
- **History**: review past sessions
